Poetry Site Template

A simple, elegant, and lightning-fast home for your words.

Core Experience

Minimalist Design

Zero distractions. Beautiful EB Garamond typography that puts your poetry center stage.

Dual Soul

Automatic Light and Dark themes to provide the perfect atmosphere for reading at any hour.

Zero Database

Powered by flat-file Markdown. No SQL setup, no complexity. Just upload and publish.

Bilingual

Native support for English and Greek, both in the interface and the content logic.

Quick Start

  1. Deploy: Upload the index.php and poems/ folder to any PHP host.
  2. Configure: Edit the top section of index.php with your name and password.
  3. Write: Create .md files using simple Markdown syntax.
